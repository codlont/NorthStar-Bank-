YOUR SECURITY MATTERS

Protecting what matters most.

Learn practical ways to protect your personal information, recognize suspicious activity, and browse financial services more safely.

Stay safer online

Good security habits help reduce the risk of identity theft, account compromise, and online fraud. These guidelines are useful whenever you use websites, mobile applications, or financial services.

🔐

Use strong passwords

Protect your online accounts with unique passwords that are difficult to guess.

  • Use a different password for every important account.
  • Choose long passwords or passphrases.
  • Consider using a trusted password manager.
  • Never share your password with another person.
🎣

Recognize phishing attempts

Fraudsters may pretend to represent a bank, company, or government agency.

  • Be cautious of unexpected messages and urgent requests.
  • Check website addresses before opening links.
  • Do not download suspicious attachments.
  • Never disclose passwords or verification codes through messages.
📱

Secure your devices

Your phone and computer should be protected against unauthorized access.

  • Install operating system and application updates.
  • Use a screen lock or biometric protection.
  • Avoid financial activity on unfamiliar public devices.
  • Only install applications from trusted sources.
👁️

Monitor account activity

Review your financial activity regularly through your actual financial institution.

  • Review transactions and account notifications.
  • Look out for unfamiliar payments or withdrawals.
  • Contact your financial institution using verified contact details.
  • Report suspicious activity promptly.
🌐

Browse carefully

Make sure you are visiting the correct website before sharing information.

  • Type website addresses carefully.
  • Look for HTTPS, but remember it does not prove a website is trustworthy.
  • Avoid links from unknown senders.
  • Do not use financial services through suspicious websites.
🛡️

Protect personal information

Keep sensitive information private and share it only when necessary.

  • Do not publish identity documents online.
  • Keep verification codes private.
  • Be careful about information shared on social media.
  • Shred or securely dispose of documents containing personal details.

What to do if you suspect fraud

If you believe your real financial account has been compromised, contact the financial institution through its verified official website or telephone number. Ask it to secure your account and follow its instructions. If money has been lost, report the incident to the appropriate authorities.

Do not rely on telephone numbers or links sent by someone who contacted you unexpectedly.
